## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-contrast luxury, precision retail curation, and tactile architectural restraint. The identity merges sleek obsidian darkness with high-energy radiant gold highlights, cultivating an atmosphere of exclusivity, performance, and intentional curation. It serves modern connoisseurs and discerning buyers who expect seamless navigation alongside uncompromising visual prestige.

The style draws heavily from modern minimalism and low-contrast structural outlines, accentuated by deliberate micro-interactions. Surfaces remain deep, clean, and distraction-free, letting product photography and elevated goods command visual dominance while bold typographic scales anchor functional transactional workflows.

## Colors

The system uses a dark-first architectural canvas centered around pitch blacks, precision dark tones, and radiant golden accents.

- **Primary Accent (`#FACC15`)**: A vibrant golden yellow deployed deliberately for primary actions, prominent badges, focused highlights, and key transactional conversion triggers.
- **Canvas & Dark Neutral Base (`#111111`)**: The core foundational dark canvas providing an immersive, high-end gallery environment.
- **Light Auxiliary Surface (`#F4F2EC`)**: A warm, tactile bone tone reserved for inverted luxury editorial modules, receipts, drawer panels, and packaging reveals.
- **Surface Tiers & Borders**:
  - Dark Surface Elevated: `#18181B`
  - Dark Surface Container: `#222225`
  - Subtle Border Dark: `#27272A`
  - Subtle Border Light: `#E4E2DC`
- **Text & Foreground**:
  - Dark Mode High Contrast: `#FFFFFF`
  - Dark Mode Muted: `#A1A1AA`
  - Light Mode Primary: `#18181B`
  - Light Mode Muted: `#71717A`
- **Functional Semantics**:
  - Success / Delivered: `#10B981`
  - Info / Shipped: `#3B82F6`
  - Warning / Pending: `#FACC15`
  - Destructive / Cancelled: `#EF4444`

## Typography

Typography establishes an assertive geometric cadence. Headers rendered in **Plus Jakarta Sans** project confident modernity with slightly tightened letter-spacing, while body and operational elements rely on **Inter** for uncompromising optical clarity in inventory, pricing, and sizing charts.

Italics are strictly avoided across all editorial, promotional, and interface copy to maintain a clean, architectural rigidity. Numerical figures in checkout funnels and luxury pricing specifications use tabular numeral formatting (`tnum`) for exact vertical alignment.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a flexible 12-column grid system for desktop and tablet screens, reflowing into a dynamic 4-column system for mobile viewports.

- **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12 columns, `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters, anchored by outer margins up to `5rem` (`80px`) with a maximum container limit of `1440px`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, `2rem` (`32px`) margins.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px)**: 4 columns, `1rem` (`16px`) gutters, `1rem` (`16px`) margins.

Structural rhythm follows an 8pt base scale with 4pt half-steps (`0.25rem` / `4px`) for dense transactional indicators. Large merchandising panels utilize generous section spacing (`space-3xl`) to establish high-fashion pacing between content modules.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is achieved through disciplined surface-container layering and razor-sharp perimeter borders (`#27272A`), rather than heavy diffuse drop shadows.

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base)**: Flat `#111111` backdrop.
- **Level 1 (Card & Section Container)**: Solid `#18181B` surface paired with a 1px border of `#27272A`. Zero ambient blur.
- **Level 2 (Hover / Elevated Floating Panels)**: `#222225` surface accompanied by a subtle ambient shadow: `0px 12px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)` and an intensified border highlight (`#3F3F46`).
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays & Drawers)**: `#18181B` with a dual-layer shadow: `0px 24px 60px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75)` and `0px 2px 0px rgba(250, 204, 21, 0.15)` along top edges.
- **Tactile High-Contrast Accent Glow**: Interactive primary elements leverage an active micro-glow: `0px 0px 18px rgba(250, 204, 21, 0.25)` to emphasize purchase intent.

## Shapes

The system standardizes on balanced corner geometry (level `2`), establishing a modern architectural aesthetic that feels polished without sacrificing structural tension.

- **Standard Base Radii (`0.5rem` / `8px`)**: Applied across standard buttons, text input fields, product thumbnail containers, and contextual popovers.
- **Large Container Radii (`1rem` / `16px`)**: Applied to high-end product display cards, modal sheets, and checkout modules.
- **Full Pill Radii (`9999px`)**: Reserved exclusively for status indicators, inventory badges, and micro-filter chips to provide geometric contrast against square product framing.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Background `#FACC15`, text `#111111`, font `label-lg`, height `48px`, border radius `8px`. Hover: Background `#EAB308` with a 2px lift and subtle gold glow. Active: Scale `0.99`.
- **Secondary Dark**: Background `#18181B`, border `1px solid #27272A`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover: Border `#A1A1AA`, background `#222225`.
- **Ghost Action**: Transparent background, text `#FFFFFF`, hover background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.

### Product & Editorial Cards
- Constructed with `#18181B` surface and `1px solid #27272A`. Corner radius `16px`.
- Media aspect ratios strictly locked to `1:1` or `4:5` with overflow hidden.
- Hover transition reveals subtle image zoom (`1.03`), elevates the surface border to `#3F3F46`, and animates a floating quick-add gold button.

### Badges & Status Chips
- Height `24px`, fully rounded pill (`9999px`), uppercase tracking `label-caps`.
- **Pending / Processing**: Background `rgba(250, 204, 21, 0.12)`, text `#FACC15`, border `1px solid rgba(250, 204, 21, 0.3)`.
- **Delivered / In Stock**: Background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, text `#10B981`, border `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`.
- **Shipped / Transit**: Background `rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.12)`, text `#3B82F6`, border `1px solid rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.3)`.
- **Cancelled / Depleted**: Background `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.12)`, text `#EF4444`, border `1px solid rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.3)`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Height `48px`, background `#111111`, border `1px solid #27272A`, color `#FFFFFF`, placeholder `#71717A`.
- Focus state: Border color `#FACC15`, box-shadow `0px 0px 0px 1px #FACC15`.
- Error state: Border color `#EF4444`, feedback message in `body-sm` `#EF4444`.

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes**: `18px x 18px`, `4px` radius, background `#18181B`, border `1px solid #27272A`. Checked state fills `#FACC15` with an obsidian `#111111` check icon.
- **Radio Buttons**: `18px x 18px`, circular, border `1px solid #27272A`. Selected state contains an inner `#FACC15` solid disc.

### Lists & Navigation Rails
- Dense rows with subtle border dividers (`1px solid #1F1F23`).
- Selected navigation items display an active left border indicator of `3px solid #FACC15` and elevated font weight (`600`).
